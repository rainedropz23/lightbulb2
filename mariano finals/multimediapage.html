Jhené Aiko

Singer · Songwriter · Storyteller

Jhené Aiko is an American singer and songwriter known for her soft, dreamy vocals and deeply personal music. She was born Jhené Aiko Efuru Chilombo on March 16, 1988, in Los Angeles, California. She began her music career as a teenager and initially gained attention through her association with the R&B group B2K. She later established herself as a solo artist with her 2011 mixtape Sailing Soul(s), which helped introduce her unique musical style. Her breakthrough came with the 2013 EP Sail Out, which featured popular songs such as “The Worst” and “Bed Peace,” featuring Childish Gambino. She has since released several successful albums, including Souled Out (2014), Trip (2017), and Chilombo (2020). Jhené Aiko’s music combines elements of R&B, neo-soul, hip-hop, and alternative music, and her lyrics often focus on love, heartbreak, healing, self-discovery, spirituality, grief, and personal growth. Some of her well-known songs include “The Worst,” “Sativa,” “While We’re Young,” “Triggered (freestyle),” “Bed Peace,” and “From Time.” Her calm and emotional sound has made her a popular artist among listeners who enjoy reflective and relaxing music. Throughout her career, Jhené Aiko has become recognized for her distinctive voice, honest songwriting, and ability to express personal experiences through music.
